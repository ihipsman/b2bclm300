Tips for writing a custom job description

Include

  • What they'll own (expansion, retention, onboarding) and the metric it's measured by
  • Seniority and team scope: individual contributor or manager of X
  • Segment (SMB, mid-market, enterprise) and motion (PLG or sales-led)
  • 3–5 real must-haves in the box below

Leave out: noise

  • Company boilerplate, perks, benefits, EEO text
  • "Team player," "self-starter," "fast-paced"
  • Long tool lists. Name only the 2–3 that matter.

Leave out: bias

  • Age signals: "digital native," "recent grad," year caps
  • "Rockstar," "ninja," "culture fit"
  • "Native English," degree or physical requirements unless essential

Leave out: confidential

  • Unannounced products, internal revenue numbers, who's being replaced

What to leave out of your resume

Personal

  • SSN, date of birth or age, photo
  • Full home address (city or region is fine)
  • Marital status, family, religion, health
  • Salary history and references' contact info

Confidential

  • NDA-covered revenue or ARR. Use percentages instead.
  • Customers who aren't public references
  • Internal project code names
